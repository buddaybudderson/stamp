Corrections

The record of what was wrongA Budday Budderson Production

An issued Calibration is never edited. If a number changes, a correction is issued under a new date and the superseded issue stands, so the record shows what was believed and when. Corrections are findings, and they are published like findings — here, and in the next issue.

Before the first issue, the program corrected itself in its own working files more than once — a confidence interval wrong in both directions at once, a reference model chosen twice on recalled rather than measured information, a gate that passed because it was measuring nothing. Those are recorded in the issue itself under How to read this.

To report an error

Write to s@bud.day with the issue, the page or figure, and what you believe is wrong. Every report is answered; a confirmed error is issued here under its own date.
