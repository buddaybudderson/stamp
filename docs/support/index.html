    Support

    What it costs, and who is payingA Budday Budderson Production

    This program measures how AI systems behave, publishes the method and the costs, and prints its own mistakes in a public register. It is a for-profit company rather than a charity, and it says so on every page that touches money. This is where support comes in and where it is accounted for.

    Status

    This page is published before the payment links are open

    The Independence page states that a supporter is named “on this page, on the support page, and in the home footer.” Until today there was no support page, which made that a promise to a page that did not exist. It is recorded in Corrections + changes with its date, like every other defect found here.

    The payment links are not live yet. When they open, the three options below become buttons on this page and this notice is removed and logged. Until then there is one address, nine characters long: s@bud.day.

    The ask

    What support pays for, in the order it would be spent

    At full cadence — the bank fired on schedule, panels rather than a single grader, and several agent teams running at once — the program needs up to $15,000 a month in cash or API credit. That is an estimate and it is labeled as one rather than printed as a finding: there is no charged figure yet, for the reason given first below. When a measured figure exists it will replace this one, and the estimate will stay on the page beside it, so anyone can see how far off it was.

    1. A fully instrumented round, and then a price. The 2,673 draws in measurement one carry no recorded cost, because the runner learned to record spend after they were taken. The first thing money buys is a round instrumented end to end, after which this page stops describing work and starts quoting a figure.

    2. The confidence instrument. Asking a model what it would stake on an answer, and scoring that against what actually happened. It is the difference between catching a compounding failure and reporting one afterwards.

    3. The re-grade of both arms, and the judging panel. The tier-1 grader sat 4.1 points below a three-judge panel — four times the size of the effect it was meant to reveal — and in measurement one the grader cost multiples of the models it was grading. The protocol question sits queued behind its instrument, and money is what un-queues it.

    4. Publishing the run files. figures.json registers the SHA-256 of files nobody can yet download, so the digests cannot be recomputed off this site. That is stated as a real gap rather than implied away. Closing it is hosting and hours, not research.

    Readers

    Three ways to give, and nothing bought by any of them

    Once, in an amount you choose. — $5 a month. — $25 a month.

    Reader support buys no tier, no badge, no early access, no vote on what is measured, and no mention inside an issue. It buys the work continuing, and that is the whole of it. Contributions below the naming thresholds published on Independence are not named individually; anyone who would rather not appear at any level can say so and will not.

    Companies

    If this program measures your models, read the conditions first

    Support from a company whose models are measured here is accepted, under ten published conditions written before any of it was asked for. The short version: your support never pays for a measurement of you; every route and every credit is disclosed issue by issue; and there are limits on how much of a year’s resources any single supporter may represent.

    One condition is worth stating here because it is the one companies ask about. Telling this program it has made a factual error costs you nothing — a correction is welcome from anyone, checked on the merits, and published under the name of whoever raised it. Objecting to a finding that survives that check, or asking for one to be changed, softened, delayed or withdrawn, ends the support and is published in full alongside the finding. Every supporter agrees to that in writing before anything is accepted.

    The full list of companies that will be asked is published on Independence, with the answers blank, because it was published before anyone was asked. Write to s@bud.day.
