Independence

Who pays, and who may notA Budday Budderson Production

An evaluation program is only worth reading if you know who pays for it and what they get. Here is the whole answer, and the promise that any change to it is itself a disclosure.

Standing

Not affiliated, sponsored, endorsed or funded by any vendor it measures

The STAMP Protocol is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or funded by any model vendor, serving provider or gateway named in its reports. No vendor has advance sight of results, right of reply before publication, or influence over what is measured. API costs are paid by the program; The Log reports what was actually charged, never an estimate.

Any of that changing is itself a disclosure, and it will appear on this page and in the Calibration of record before any result affected by it.

Recusal

Rules that are code, not intentions

  • A judge never grades its own family. The runner refuses before the first call and seats the reserve.
  • A candidate never grades. A model being measured has an interest in the measurement.
  • An anchor is not a judge. A model measured twice for two purposes is disclosed, not excluded.
  • Graders are qualified by probe, not reputation, against the cases that have broken graders before.

Money

What support may look like, and what it may not

The program is currently paid for personally. It intends to accept reader support and named sponsorship on these terms, written down before the first dollar: a sponsor is named in The Log, in the issue in which its money was first spent, and on this page; a sponsor is never named near a finding; a sponsor that makes, serves or routes models is recused from every panel and its models are measured exactly as any other’s; no sponsor sees a result before it is published. Advertising, where it appears, is placed by a third party without knowledge of content and is confined to the pages listed in the privacy statement; it never appears on an issue.

Current sponsors: none. Current funders: the publisher.

People and machines

Who makes this

Findings are produced with AI assistance throughout, including on themselves: the graders and judges are AI models, and the analysis and drafting were produced with substantial AI assistance. A program that evaluates AI systems is itself largely built and operated by them, and a reader is entitled to weigh that. What is done about it: every number is computed from files on disk with the fingerprint of its sources; no figure may enter a document by being typed; the grader is validated by a panel rather than trusted; and errors in our own work are published rather than corrected quietly. The four names on the masthead and what each may do are on the About page.

Tools. The site and the harness were built primarily with Claude (Anthropic), bought at the public price and disclosed on the Colophon, which also explains the watermark Anthropic has embedded in its models’ text since 2 August 2026. Anthropic is a vendor this program measures or may measure. It has no sponsorship, funding, review, or endorsement relationship with this program, and using its tools does not exempt its models from any rule on this page, including recusal.
