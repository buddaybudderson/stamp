What this is

A measurement programme, not a leaderboard

The STAMP Protocol measures whether a score given to an AI model survives being taken twice. Every rate it publishes carries its interval and its conditions — which grader, which serving provider, which temperature, which bank version, how many draws — and every figure is registered with the SHA-256 of the files it was computed from. It publishes its own errors as findings. It is published by Budday Budderson Studio LLC, Albuquerque, New Mexico, on alternate Saturdays at 15:37 America/Chicago, with working notes on The Bench on Sundays and Wednesdays.

It is not an audit, a certification or a regulatory judgment, and it does not measure helpfulness, harmlessness or honesty. The current issue is The Calibration 001.

The finishes

The site changes colour with the day

The seven finishes are the classical planetary week — Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Sun for Monday to Sunday — and the day is Chicago's, set before the page paints, so two readers on two continents see the same colour. Saturday is publishing day, so Saturn is the house finish. An issue wears the finish of the day it was issued: Calibration 001 wears Sun, because its release stamp was taken twenty minutes before Saturday midnight in the wrong timezone, and the off-cycle date is meant to stay visible.

The finish tints the ground only. The accent — the bar in the mark, every measured figure — never changes. Each swatch below is drawn in its own palette, day above and night below; the outlined one is today's. The switch in the top right corner changes day and night on every page, and remembers your choice.

Our own receipts

How to check any of this

Every issue's HTML is byte-frozen and the first twelve characters of its SHA-256 are printed in the foot of every page of its PDF; the archive links the source file so you can hash it yourself. figures.json holds every citable figure with the fingerprints of its sources. An issued Calibration is never edited; corrections are findings and are published like findings.
